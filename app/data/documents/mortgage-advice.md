# Mortgage Advice Partner

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — information about our mortgage advice partner. Valid from 1 January 2026._

## Who we work with

Noorderlicht Makelaars does not give mortgage advice itself. We refer clients to Hypotheek Kompas, an independent mortgage adviser that compares more than 40 lenders. You are always free to choose a different adviser; it makes no difference to how we handle your bid.

## Introductory meeting

The first meeting with Hypotheek Kompas is free and takes about one hour. After the meeting you receive a written budget indication, which you can attach to a bid.

## Advice fee

The standard fee for full mortgage advice and mediation is €2,250. Clients of Noorderlicht Makelaars pay a reduced fee of €1,950. The fee is only charged when a mortgage offer is accepted.

Noorderlicht Makelaars receives no commission or referral fee from Hypotheek Kompas.

## How long a mortgage takes

From complete application to a binding mortgage offer usually takes two to four weeks. For self-employed applicants it can take up to six weeks, because lenders require an income statement from an accredited assessor.

## What to bring

Bring your ID, your three most recent payslips, an employer's statement, an overview of any loans or student debt, and bank statements showing your own funds.

## National Mortgage Guarantee

The adviser checks whether you qualify for the National Mortgage Guarantee (NHG), which usually gives a lower interest rate in exchange for a one-off guarantee fee. NHG limits and fees are set nationally each year and are not decided by us or by Hypotheek Kompas; ask the adviser for the current figures.

## Interest rates

We do not publish interest rates. Rates change daily and differ per lender, fixed-rate period and loan-to-value ratio. Hypotheek Kompas provides a personal overview of current rates during the introductory meeting.
