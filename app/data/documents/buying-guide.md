# Buying Guide

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — step-by-step guide for buyers. Last reviewed January 2026._

## Step 1: Know your budget

Before you start viewing, get a written budget indication from a mortgage adviser. Sellers and their agents take a bid more seriously when it comes with a budget indication, and we ask for one before we submit a bid on your behalf.

Count on additional buyer's costs of roughly 4% to 6% of the purchase price on top of the price itself. These cover transfer tax, the notary, the valuation report, mortgage advice and any buying-agent fee.

## Step 2: Search and view

We set up a search profile with you and send matching homes as soon as they are listed, often one or two days before they appear on public portals. You can view as many homes as you like; see the Viewings and Bidding document for how viewings work.

## Step 3: Make a bid

A bid states the price, the proposed transfer date and any conditions. The most common conditions are a financing condition and a structural survey condition. A bid without conditions is stronger but carries more risk for you.

## Step 4: Purchase agreement and cooling-off period

Once buyer and seller agree, the selling agent draws up a written purchase agreement. The sale is only binding once both parties have signed it.

After the buyer receives the signed purchase agreement there is a statutory cooling-off period of three days, during which the buyer may cancel without giving a reason and without penalty. At least two of these three days must be working days.

## Step 5: Deposit or bank guarantee

Within five weeks of signing, the buyer provides a deposit or bank guarantee of 10% of the purchase price to the notary. If the buyer fails to complete the purchase without a valid condition, the seller is entitled to this 10% as a penalty.

## Step 6: Financing condition

The financing condition gives the buyer time to arrange a mortgage. Our standard term for the financing condition is six weeks from the date of signing. If the mortgage is refused within that term, the buyer can cancel the purchase by sending documented proof of at least one refusal from a recognised lender.

## Step 7: Structural survey

We recommend a structural survey for every home built before 1990. A survey costs between €400 and €600 depending on the size of the home and is carried out by an independent surveyor. Our standard survey condition allows the buyer to cancel if the survey shows immediately necessary repairs above €7,500.

## Step 8: Transfer at the notary

In the Netherlands the buyer chooses and pays the notary. On the transfer date you do a final inspection of the home, then sign the deed of transfer and the mortgage deed at the notary's office. You receive the keys straight after signing. A typical period between signing the purchase agreement and transfer is two to three months.
