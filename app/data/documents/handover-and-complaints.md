# Handover, Aftercare and Complaints

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — what happens at and after the transfer, and how to complain. Valid from 1 January 2026._

## Final inspection

On the day of transfer, shortly before the appointment at the notary, buyer and seller walk through the home together with our agent. We check that the home is empty and clean, that agreed items have been left behind, and that nothing has been damaged since the purchase agreement was signed.

## Meter readings and keys

During the final inspection the agent records the meter readings for gas, electricity and water on a handover form signed by both parties. All keys are handed over at the notary's office immediately after the deed of transfer is signed.

## Aftercare

Buyers can call our aftercare line for six months after the transfer with questions about the home, the handover form, or defects discovered after moving in. We help you assess whether a defect may be the seller's responsibility, but we cannot give legal advice.

## Hidden defects

If you discover a defect that the seller knew about and did not disclose, report it to the seller in writing as soon as possible, preferably within two months of discovery. Keep photos and any contractor reports.

## Complaints procedure

If you are unhappy with our service, send your complaint to klachten@noorderlicht-makelaars.example. The office manager confirms receipt within two working days and gives a substantive reply within ten working days.

If we cannot resolve the complaint together, you can submit it to the Geschillencommissie Makelaardij, the independent disputes committee for estate agents. Its decisions are binding on us.

## Reviews

After every completed sale, purchase or rental we ask for a review. Reviews are published unedited; we only remove personal data.
