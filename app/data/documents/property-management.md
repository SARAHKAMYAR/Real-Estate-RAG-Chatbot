# Property Management for Landlords

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — service description for landlords. Valid from 1 January 2026._

## What the service covers

Our property management service takes over the day-to-day running of a rental home: rent collection, tenant contact, repairs, inspections and the annual rent increase.

## Management fee

The management fee is 6% of the monthly rent, excluding VAT, with a minimum of €75 per month per home. There is no set-up fee. Landlords with five or more homes under management pay a reduced fee of 5%.

## Rent collection and payout

Rent is collected from tenants on the first of the month. We pay the rent, less the management fee and any repair costs, to the landlord by the fifth working day of each month, together with a statement.

If a tenant is more than 14 days late, we send a formal reminder. After 30 days of arrears we consult the landlord about handing the matter to a bailiff.

## Repairs

We may carry out repairs costing up to €250 per repair without asking the landlord first. For anything above €250 we request a quote and the landlord's approval, except in emergencies where delay would cause further damage.

We work with a fixed pool of local contractors and pass their invoices on at cost, without a surcharge.

## Inspections

Each managed home is inspected twice a year. The landlord receives a report with photos within one week of each inspection. Tenants are given at least seven days' notice of an inspection.

## Check-in and check-out

At the start and end of every tenancy we draw up an inspection report with photos, signed by the tenant. The check-out report is the basis for settling the security deposit.

## Ending the service

Either party can end the management agreement with two calendar months' notice. There is no termination fee.
