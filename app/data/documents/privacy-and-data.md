# Privacy and Personal Data

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — summary of our privacy policy. Valid from 1 January 2026._

## What we collect

For a viewing request we collect your name, phone number and email address. We never ask for a copy of your ID to book a viewing.

For a rental application or a bid we collect the documents needed to assess it, such as income details and a masked copy of your ID.

## How long we keep data

Viewing requests are deleted 12 months after the viewing.

Documents from rental applicants who did not get the home are deleted within four weeks of the decision.

Files of completed sales, purchases and tenancies are kept for seven years, which is the statutory retention period for our business records.

## Identity checks

When you buy or sell through us we are legally obliged to verify your identity under anti-money-laundering rules (Wwft). This check happens before the purchase agreement is signed, not at the viewing stage.

## Sharing data

We share personal data only with parties that need it for the transaction: the notary, the other party's agent, and, with your permission, the mortgage adviser. We never sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by emailing privacy@noorderlicht-makelaars.example. We respond within 30 days.

## Chat assistant

This fictional demo does not save conversations in an application database. The browser keeps recent messages in memory until the page is refreshed or the chat is cleared. Questions, recent messages and retrieved passages are sent to the configured model provider to generate answers. If web search is enabled, search queries are sent to Tavily. Those services have their own data-handling policies; this demo does not control their retention. Do not share personal, financial, identity or confidential information in this demo.
