# Viewings and Bidding

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — house rules for viewings and the bidding process. Valid from 1 January 2026._

## Booking a viewing

Viewings are booked through the listing page or by phone, at least 24 hours in advance. Viewings take place Monday to Saturday; we do not hold viewings on Sundays or public holidays.

Each viewing slot lasts 30 minutes and is always accompanied by one of our agents. A second viewing of the same home may last up to 60 minutes, and you are welcome to bring a surveyor or contractor.

## Cancelling or rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least four hours before the appointment. After two no-shows without notice, we reserve the right to decline further viewing requests.

## Open house days

For homes with high interest we organise an open house instead of individual slots. Registration is still required, and a maximum of eight visitors are admitted per 30-minute slot.

## How bidding works

All bids are submitted in writing through our online bidding portal, Open Bod. We do not accept bids by phone or in person. Every bidder receives a confirmation with a timestamp, and after the sale each bidder can request an anonymised bidding log showing all bids and when they were received.

## Bidding deadline

When more than one serious bid is expected, the selling agent sets a bidding deadline. This is a single sealed round: each bidder makes one best and final bid before the deadline, and bidders are not told the amount of other bids. Deadlines are normally set at 12:00 noon on a working day.

## What a complete bid contains

A complete bid states the price, the preferred transfer date, any conditions (for example financing or structural survey), and a budget indication from a mortgage adviser or proof of own funds.

## The seller's decision

The seller is free to choose any bid and is not obliged to accept the highest one. Conditions and the transfer date often weigh as heavily as price. We inform all bidders of the outcome within one working day of the deadline.

## Fair treatment

We follow the same procedure for every bidder. Agents of Noorderlicht Makelaars and their immediate family are not allowed to bid on homes we list.
