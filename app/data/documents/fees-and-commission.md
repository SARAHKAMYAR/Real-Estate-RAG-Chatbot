# Fees and Commission

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — client fee schedule, valid from 1 January 2026. All amounts include 21% VAT unless stated otherwise._

## Selling a home

Our selling commission is 1.35% of the final sale price, with a minimum fee of €3,950. We work on a no-cure-no-pay basis: the commission is only due when the sale completes at the notary.

A one-off start-up fee of €495 covers professional photography, a measured floor plan and the listing on Funda. The start-up fee is charged when the listing goes live and is not refunded if the home does not sell.

If a seller withdraws the home from the market before a sale is agreed, we charge a withdrawal fee of €950 to cover marketing costs already made. No withdrawal fee applies after twelve months on the market.

## Buying a home

Our buying-agent service has a fixed fee of €3,450, regardless of the purchase price. The fee covers the search profile, unlimited viewings with an agent, the bidding strategy, negotiation and review of the purchase agreement.

The buying-agent fee is due only when a purchase agreement is signed. If you stop the search before buying, you pay nothing.

A stand-alone purchase-agreement review, without the rest of the buying service, costs €395.

## Renting

Tenants never pay an agency fee or mediation fee to Noorderlicht Makelaars. This applies to every rental listing we advertise.

Landlords pay a finder's fee equal to one month's rent, excluding VAT, when a tenancy agreement is signed. The finder's fee includes tenant screening, the tenancy agreement and the check-in inspection report.

## Valuations

A free valuation for sellers is an indicative estimate of market value and costs nothing.

A certified valuation report for a mortgage lender (NWWI-validated) costs €695 and is carried out by an independent registered valuer, not by the agent who is selling or buying the home.

## Payment terms

Invoices are payable within 14 days. Commission on a sale is normally settled through the notary on the transfer date, so sellers do not need to pay it up front.
