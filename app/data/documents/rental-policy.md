# Rental Policy for Tenants

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — how renting through us works. Valid from 1 January 2026._

## Income requirement

To qualify for a rental home, your gross monthly income must be at least 3.5 times the monthly basic rent. For two applicants renting together, the second income counts in full.

Self-employed applicants qualify on the average net profit of the last two calendar years, shown by annual accounts or tax assessments.

## Guarantors

If your income is below the requirement, a guarantor is accepted. The guarantor must live in the Netherlands and have a gross monthly income of at least 5 times the monthly rent. One guarantor per tenancy agreement is permitted.

Students may rent with a guarantor on listings that are marked as student-friendly.

## Documents we ask for

An application is complete when we have received: a copy of a valid ID with the citizen service number masked, an employment contract or employer's statement, the three most recent payslips, and a landlord reference or mortgage statement for your current address.

We respond to every complete application within three working days.

## Deposit

The security deposit is two months' basic rent, which is the legal maximum. The deposit is returned within 14 days after the end of the tenancy when there is no damage or arrears, and within 30 days when costs are deducted.

## Lease term

Our standard tenancy agreement is for an indefinite period with a minimum first term of 12 months. After the first 12 months the tenant can end the tenancy with one calendar month's notice.

## Pets

Pets are allowed only where the listing states so. For homes that allow pets, we ask tenants to register the pet in the tenancy agreement; there is no extra pet deposit.

## Smoking and subletting

Smoking inside the home is not allowed in any rental we manage. Subletting, including short-stay holiday rental through online platforms, is prohibited without written permission from the landlord.

## Rent increases

Rent is indexed once a year on 1 July, in line with the statutory maximum that applies to the type of tenancy. Tenants receive written notice of the new rent at least two months in advance.

## Agency fees

Tenants do not pay any agency, mediation or administration fee. See the Fees and Commission document for the fees that landlords pay.
