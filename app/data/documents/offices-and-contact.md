# Offices and Contact

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — office details and how to reach us. Updated January 2026._

## Utrecht office

Our head office is at Oudegracht 212, 3511 NR Utrecht. Phone: 030 555 0142. Email: utrecht@noorderlicht-makelaars.example.

Opening hours in Utrecht are Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:30, and Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00.

## Amsterdam office

Our Amsterdam office is at Prinsengracht 480, 1017 KG Amsterdam. Phone: 020 555 0187. Email: amsterdam@noorderlicht-makelaars.example.

Opening hours in Amsterdam are Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:30. The Amsterdam office is closed on Saturdays; Saturday viewings in Amsterdam are by appointment only.

## Rotterdam

We do not have an office in Rotterdam. Listings in Rotterdam are handled by the team at the Utrecht office.

## Response times

We answer emails and website enquiries within one working day. Phone calls that we miss are returned the same working day when received before 16:00.

## Languages

Our agents speak Dutch and English. German-speaking clients can ask for an agent at the Utrecht office. Purchase and tenancy agreements are drawn up in Dutch; we provide an English courtesy translation on request at no extra cost.

## Emergency repairs for tenants

Tenants of homes that we manage can reach the emergency repair line 24 hours a day on 030 555 0199. The line is for urgent problems only, such as a burst pipe, a gas smell, or no heating in winter. Non-urgent repairs are reported through the tenant portal and are picked up within two working days.

## Holidays

Both offices are closed on Dutch public holidays and between Christmas and New Year's Day. The emergency repair line stays open during these periods.
