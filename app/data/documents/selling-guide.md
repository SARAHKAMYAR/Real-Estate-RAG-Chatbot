# Selling Guide

_Noorderlicht Makelaars — what to expect when you sell with us. Last reviewed January 2026._

## Free valuation

Every sale starts with a free valuation visit. An agent visits your home, and you receive a written valuation with a recommended asking price within 48 hours of the visit. There is no obligation to list with us afterwards.

## Preparing the listing

After you sign the listing agreement we schedule the photo shoot. Your home goes live on Funda and on our own website within five working days after the photo shoot.

A valid energy label is legally required before a home can be advertised. If you do not have one, we arrange an energy-label inspection through a certified adviser for €285.

## Optional home staging

Our home staging package costs €750 and includes a styling consultation, rental furniture and accessories for up to six weeks, and a second photo session. Staging is optional and is charged separately from the start-up fee.

## Asking price strategy

We advise on one of two strategies: a realistic asking price with room for negotiation, or a sharp "bidding from" price designed to attract several bidders. Your agent explains which strategy suits your home and the current local market.

## Time to sell

In 2025 the homes we sold found a buyer in an average of 27 days from the date they went live. Apartments in Utrecht sold fastest, at an average of 19 days.

## During the sale

You receive a weekly report with the number of online views, viewing requests and feedback from viewers. Your agent is present at every viewing; sellers are asked not to be at home during viewings.

## Seller's duty to disclose

Sellers must complete a property information questionnaire and disclose known defects, such as leaks, subsidence or asbestos. Hiding a known defect can make the seller liable after the sale.

## After a bid is accepted

We draw up the purchase agreement within three working days of an accepted bid. See the Buying Guide for the cooling-off period and the deposit that the buyer provides, and the Fees and Commission document for what selling costs.
